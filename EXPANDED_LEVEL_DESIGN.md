# Expanded Level Design - Treasure Excavator

## Document Information
- **Version**: 2.0
- **Last Updated**: 2025-11-19
- **Purpose**: Extended level progression with 20 levels across 4 worlds

---

## Level Progression Overview

### World Structure
- **4 Worlds** with 5 levels each (20 total levels)
- Each world introduces new mechanics and challenges
- Difficulty curve: Easy → Medium → Hard → Expert

### World Themes

#### World 1: Desert Excavation (Levels 1-5)
- **Theme**: Sandy desert mining site
- **Visual**: Golden sand, cacti, rock formations
- **New Mechanics**: Basic collection, gates, deposits
- **Color Palette**: Yellow, orange, brown

#### World 2: Mountain Quarry (Levels 6-10)
- **Theme**: Rocky mountain mining operation
- **Visual**: Gray rocks, snow peaks, pine trees
- **New Mechanics**: Moving obstacles, ice patches (slippery)
- **Color Palette**: Gray, white, dark blue

#### World 3: Jungle Ruins (Levels 11-15)
- **Theme**: Ancient temple excavation
- **Visual**: Vines, stone temples, waterfalls
- **New Mechanics**: Rotating platforms, bouncy obstacles
- **Color Palette**: Green, brown, gold

#### World 4: Volcanic Mine (Levels 16-20)
- **Theme**: Active volcano mining
- **Visual**: Lava flows, obsidian, geysers
- **New Mechanics**: Hazards (lava), explosive obstacles, time pressure
- **Color Palette**: Red, orange, black

## Bonus Levels (Post-Game Content)

### Endless Mode
- Procedurally generated levels
- Increasing difficulty
- Leaderboards
- Special rewards at milestones (500, 1000, 2500, 5000, 10000 points)

### Challenge Modes

#### Time Attack
- Complete levels as fast as possible
- Ghost data from personal best
- Online leaderboards

#### Perfect Run
- Complete without taking damage
- No combo breaks allowed
- Huge score multiplier

#### Minimal Gates
- Complete using 3 or fewer gates
- Strategic treasure collection required

#### Cargo Master
- Never exceed 80% capacity
- Frequent deposits required
- Tests efficiency
