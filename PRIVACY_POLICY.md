## 4. iOS 14+ App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

In compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework:

- **We will ask for your permission** before tracking you across apps and websites owned by other companies
- You can **grant or deny** this permission at any time in Settings > Privacy > Tracking
- **Denying permission** will not affect app functionality—you can still play the full game
- If you deny tracking, you will see **non-personalized ads** instead of personalized ads

**What changes if you opt out:**
- You still see ads, but they are generic (not based on your interests)
- We still collect anonymized analytics for app improvement
- Your game progress is still saved
